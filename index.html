Clear a day, not a decade

Clearing a chat in WhatsApp is all-or-nothing today: you keep everything, or you lose everything. This concept adds one screen in between — pick a day, or a range of days, and clear only that. Eight frames, click through them below.

    The three new screens are the scope chooser, the date picker, and the confirmation with its live tally. Everything else already exists in the app — which is the point: the feature is an insert, not a rebuild.
