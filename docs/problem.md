# Problem

## The Opportunity

WhatsApp's existing chat-clearing experience creates a gap between two choices: keeping the conversation history and clearing it.

This concept explores a third option:

> **Clear a day, not a decade.**

Instead of clearing the entire chat, users can choose a specific past day or a custom date range and clear only that portion of the conversation.

## The Design Question

**What if users could clear messages from a specific day or date range without clearing the rest of their chat history?**

The goal of this project was to explore how that capability could fit naturally into the existing Clear Chat experience.

## Why Date-Based Clearing?

A date or range gives users more control over the scope of a clearing action.

The concept allows users to:

- Select a particular day
- Select a start and end date
- Review the exact range before clearing
- Keep older or newer history outside the selected range

## Existing vs. Proposed Experience

### Existing Clear Chat

- Clears all messages at once
- Provides no date-based scope
- Does not provide an Undo option

### Proposed Clear by Date

- Allows a specific date or custom range
- Clearly communicates the selected scope
- Includes a short Undo window after clearing

## Scope

The first version of this concept focuses on one-to-one chats.

Group chats are intentionally left for separate consideration because their shared history introduces additional product and interaction questions.
